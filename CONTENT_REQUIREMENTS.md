## 1. Images

### What makes a good website photo

- **Format:** JPG, the original file straight from the camera or photographer, not a screenshot or WhatsApp copy (those are compressed).
- **Size:** at least 2400 pixels on the longest side for large images, and at least 1600 pixels for everything else.
- **Orientation:** as listed below. Portrait = taller than wide, landscape = wider than tall.
- **Light and mood:** natural light, calm and uncluttered, matching the soft peach, green and gold brand.
- **Rights:** you must own each photo or have permission to use it (see section 1.6).
